Smart Greenhouse Automation System

Personal project

ESP32 · MQTT · Google Blockly · DHT22 · soil moisture · reservoir level · OLED

  • No-code IoT rule engine that monitors greenhouse conditions and automates operations. Rules are built by dragging Blockly blocks, not writing code.
  • Reads temperature, humidity, soil moisture and reservoir level, with live status on an OLED display.
  • Controls a water pump and ventilation from the rules. Rules are validated, run in real time, and can be saved and loaded as profiles.
  • Data flows from sensors over MQTT to a dashboard, then through the Blockly rule engine to the actuators.

ESP32-CAM · PIR · ultrasonic · Telegram Bot API · L298N

IR Obstacle Detection with Email Alerts

Personal project

Arduino Uno · NodeMCU · IR sensors · LCD · SMTP

  • IR sensors detect an obstacle, and an Arduino Uno with a NodeMCU handle the detection and the alert.
